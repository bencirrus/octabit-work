Where do you spend time on tedious work? What do you have to do repeatedly that can't be done exactly the same way to be scripted?

Its probabilistic nature makes it predictably good at some types of knowledge work: summarizing, drafting, classifying, extracting, and generating options.

That also makes it unpredictable at correctly answering small context specifics of, say, what to do with the @ symbol in your business name when the IRS online form rejects it as you apply for an EIN.

Assessing "AI maturity" is useful eventually, but delays action. We learn more by using it.

Get ahead of the inevitable installations, agentic action, and data leakage because it's a wild west out there with apps, services, and code that look legit but may not be or may do unintended damage.
