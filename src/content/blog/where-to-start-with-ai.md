description: Plan first or start using
pubDate: 2026-06-04T16:25:00.000-07:00
category: advisory
---
Where do you spend time on tedious work? Where does information get stuck moving between people or systems? What do you have to do repeatedly?

It is good at the specific knowledge work: summarizing, drafting, classifying, extracting, and generating options. 
  
Assessing "AI maturity" is useful eventually, but delays action. We learn more by using it.

Get ahead of the inevitable installations, agentic action and data leakage because it's a wild west out there with apps, services and code that look legit but may not be.
