Where do you spend time on tedious work? What do you have to do repeatedly but not exactly the same way, say in a way that could be scripted?

The probabilistic nature of it makes it predictably good at some knowledge work: summarizing, drafting, classifying, extracting, and generating options. 

That also makes it unpredictable at correctly answering small context specifics of, say what to do with a common symbol like @ in your business name when applying for an EIN with the IRS.
  
Assessing "AI maturity" is useful eventually, but delays action. We learn more by using it.

Get ahead of the inevitable installations, agentic action and data leakage because it's a wild west out there with apps, services and code that look legit but may not be.
